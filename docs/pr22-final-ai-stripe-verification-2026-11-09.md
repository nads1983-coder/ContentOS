## Cost and profitability

The **£0.36864 per fully active customer/month** and **£9.216 for 25** remain arithmetically defensible conditional *planning figures*, not verified outcomes or enforceable production maxima. They assume 50 delivered generations each, two maximum-cost attempts each, per attempt ≤8,192 input plus ≤4,096 output tokens, no tools or hidden history, standard tier, Luna input charged at 1.25× cache-write pricing, £1/USD and 20% contingency. At [official GPT-6 Luna rates](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-luna) of $0.10 input and $0.50 output per million, this is £0.0036864/attempt or £0.0073728/delivered credit. Model access, quality, exact payload accounting, token caps and durable retry limits are not established in production. No invoice evidence was available. Provider price changes, taxes, other calls, uncertain provider charges and activity outside the application ledger remain risks.

At the same **unimplemented** assumptions, and using 20% VAT-inclusive prices, [Stripe's published](https://stripe.com/gb/pricing) 5.15% international-card/FX stress fee plus £0.20 rounded up, and a 10%-of-gross operating reserve, projected contributions are £3.022/£5.561/£9.283 for the £5/25, £9/50 and £15/100 packages: 72.5%/74.2%/74.3% of VAT-exclusive revenue. These are scenarios; actual fee mix, taxation, refund rates, dispute fees, future prices, non-expiring credit liability and expenses are unverified. Therefore all three packages remain **not approved for activation**.

At the current GPT-5.2 code-default quote (132,096 input estimate and 24,000 output tokens), the approximate successful-request reservation is £0.680602, so 50 requests require £34.03 and 25 fully active lifetime customers £850.75 per month before retries and existing demand. This is a conservative application quote, not proof of the deployed model or a provider-enforced hard input cap. The £30 monthly application gate means those calls cannot all be admitted; it is not funding for the promise. £30 does not bound unrelated provider-key use outside the application.
