**Scope:** Only this directory's `brief.json` and `raw-draft.json`. Inspected all 14 formats, pack title/summary, all 57 top-level items and every nested repurposed asset. No execution logs, validator implementation, other assessments, network, provider, database or deployment access.

**Methodological disclosure:** The assignment explicitly highlighted the page-layout ambiguity, so this was not a fully blinded assessment. The remaining assessment was independent.

**There is no final validated pack. This is a diagnostic assessment of RAW copy, not evidence of successful delivery.**

## Factual Verdict

**Material failure: an unconfirmed physical page layout is presented as fact.**

The brief says: “Its new notebook, Fieldline, has an A5 paper format and three printed section headings: Notice, Sketch, and Revisit.” It also says: “These are the only confirmed product specifications.”

**A5 format and three printed section headings do NOT establish that all three headings are printed on every individual page.** They also do not establish that all three share one page or that individual pages are divided into three areas. The raw claims are unsupported, not proven physically false; the actual arrangement remains unknown.

There are **10 material findings across 8 formats**, all manifestations of this one issue: eight explicit per-page findings and two further layout assertions. The full video's spoken and on-screen versions are grouped as one finding, not counted twice.

## Distinctions That Matter

- **Supported:** product/studio names, A5 format, moss-green cover, heading names and intended informal-observation audience. No price, page count, paper weight, binding, shipping, testimonial or measured result was invented.
- **Old offer:** no waterproof/weather-resistant claim appears. Imaginary outdoor settings do not establish or claim weather resistance.
- **Duration:** the assets omit A Page Outside, Rowan and the 28-minute event. No duration-to-outcome promise or invented event activity was found. Product awareness is the requested central goal, so omission is not itself a factual failure.
- **Allowed creativity:** the explicitly imaginary square, fountain sounds, sketches and unfinished notes are not customer experiences or announced curriculum. “Calm”, “without guilt”, “permission” and “still a complete page” are subjective framing, not demonstrated wellbeing benefits.
- **Ambiguity, not another definite fabrication:** the X post's “built-in place” could mean a metaphor or an actual reserved area. Instagram's use of the three headings does not, by itself, establish an every-page claim. Clarify such wording without assuming the product has a particular layout.
- **No CTA:** no listed commercial/social solicitation or engagement question was found; all CTA fields and the CTA set are empty. “Try one line” and Fill/Add/Leave steps are notebook-use guidance. A literal ban on every directive would also exclude them; optional hypothetical wording is the safest edit. This interpretation risk is not counted as a confirmed engagement-CTA failure.
