The dated current source overrides the saved offer. Saved brand name and audience do not conflict. Use the following as a relationship checklist, not a keyword whitelist:

An omitted detail is not automatically a factual error. Each format may select a useful subset. However, an affirmative statement must keep the qualifications needed to make that statement true: "eighteen covers per range" is false, whereas a short post about Arc's removable outer cover need not also give every collection date. Judge ordinary language in context; "machine-washable Arc outer cover" need not repeat the temperature in every hook, but "wash the whole cushion" changes the object of the instruction.

Explicitly allowed paraphrases and creative choices include "four Arc patterns and eight Bond patterns", "a shared eighteen-cover ceiling", "the sample envelope is posted; covers are collected", "orders open on 20 November", and "a little texture around a familiar cushion". Dates may omit the year when the dated campaign context remains unambiguous. Neutral availability is not an imperative. "Arc's zip gives access to the removable outer cover" is supported; "a zip that makes cleaning twice as fast" invents an outcome. "Texture can be a quiet accent" is subjective, not a measured claim. "Consider the care label when comparing covers" is qualified content advice, not an engagement CTA. A rhetorical explanation is not automatically an engagement question; "Which would you choose?" or "Tell us your favourite" solicits participation and violates the brief. Fictional imagery must not masquerade as a named or first-person customer endorsement.

## Five Dimensions, Each 0-4

Score each of the fourteen available assets independently. Total is the sum of fidelity, usefulness, originality, brandVoice and ctaCompliance, maximum 20. Scores and critical defects are separate: a high total cannot cancel a material factual or CTA error. Preserve exact excerpts, field paths and the contradicted or missing source fact for each defect. Do not infer the model's intentions.

| Dimension | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fidelity | All assertions supported or clearly creative/subjective; important relationships preserved. | Accurate with a minor ambiguity that does not create a material false belief. | A consequential ambiguity or qualification loss needs editing, but no definite material false claim. | One definite material unsupported/contradicted fact or promise. | Multiple material false claims or the central proposition is false. |
| Usefulness | Complete, usable format with a clear audience-relevant angle and appropriate detail. | Usable with light editing. | Significant rewriting, repetition or incomplete format utility. | Mostly generic, operator instructions or very incomplete. | Unusable, incoherent or functionally empty. |
| Originality | Distinct angle, structure and phrasing adapted to this format without inventions. | Clear adaptation with some familiar phrasing. | Mostly source restatement or repetitive across assets. | Near-copy/template with little adaptation. | No meaningful audience-facing adaptation. |
| Brand voice | Consistently conversational, balanced, calm and concrete. | Minor stiffness or generic wording. | Noticeably uneven, overlong or promotional. | Sustained hype, pressure, jargon or unsuitable persona. | Fundamentally incompatible or misleading brand persona. |
| CTA compliance | No engagement/purchase directive; neutral statements and genuine content advice only. | Awkward but non-directive ending or unnecessary CTA-related metadata. | Ambiguous invitation that requires editing; no definite directive. | One clear forbidden request or engagement question. | Multiple or central forbidden directives, fabricated destination or contact route. |

No-CTA structural exception: the dedicated `ctas` section must contain zero items, an empty CTA field, and only a short neutral no-CTA explanation in its body. Its usefulness/originality are judged against that constrained purpose; do not penalize it for not offering creative calls to action. Concise intentional absence is a correct result. A normal empty CTA field elsewhere is also correct. Standard filming directions in a video script and required repurpose/hashtag labels are structural material, not operator meta-copy. "Here are captions you can post" in a social post is operator meta-copy.

Inspect title/summary claims as well as the fourteen sections. A pack-level falsehood is a pack-level critical defect; do not multiply it into fourteen invented independent errors. Count both distinct underlying defects and affected formats if one falsehood is repeated. Structural failures should name the affected type and actual missing/extra elements, not just say "bad formatting".

## Critical Defects, False Positives and Release Judgment

Mark a material unsupported/contradicted product, price, scope, location, quantity, date, capability, result or fulfilment promise as critical. A clear prohibited CTA or engagement request is also critical. A near-copy can be low-originality without being ungrounded; distinguish that from invented facts. Minor stylistic weaknesses do not become safety failures merely because the test is demanding.

A false-positive grounding block requires evidence that the exact blocked claim was entailed or plainly allowed creative/subjective language. Quote that claim and its supporting source. A timeout, budget cap, missing output or uninformative refusal is a delivery/availability failure, not proof of a semantic false positive. Do not score an unseen draft or assume a repaired final output contains the original draft's defect. If a repair strips useful supported facts or replaces a usable asset with generic filler, record the final usefulness loss separately from factual safety.

Provisional copy-quality acceptance requires all fourteen delivered formats, no critical defects anywhere, valid format structure, fidelity and CTA compliance at least 3 for every asset, usefulness at least 3 for every asset, and a mean total of at least 15/20. These are frozen operational thresholds for this case, not statistical superiority claims. An unavailable/withheld pack cannot pass end-to-end acceptance even if a supplied raw draft scores well. A passing pack supports only this limited case; it does not prove arbitrary natural-language grounding, general production readiness or authorise release by itself.

Report: execution/delivery status; fourteen scored or explicitly missing rows; concrete defect excerpts with reasons; any evidenced false-positive blocks; per-dimension means over scored assets only; and a short pass, fail or not-executed verdict with limitations. A single candidate run provides no baseline regression estimate. Do not make paired or causal claims without a genuinely paired evaluation.
