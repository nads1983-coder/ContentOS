## Root causes and changes

1. **Classification contract drift.** The reviewer guessed which content was formatting. Every review unit now carries the exact structural eligibility enforced by the server. Platform prefixes do not exempt headlines. The actual six-item X thread validates both ordinal and total, including LF/CRLF separators. Copied, forged or mutated annotations cannot confer exemptions. Script beats and nested ordered lists are checked against the actual sequence.
2. **Words and citations mistaken for relationships.** A bounded claim-slot guard preserves named subject, part/location, property, quantity bounds, distribution, frequency and guarantee modality for recognized assertions. Notebook headings do not establish page headings, every-page headings, or page divisions. Aggregate counts across rooms do not prove a count in every room; a three-to-five range does not prove exactly three. Unresolved pronouns and unrecognized relationships require semantic review, not guessed deterministic approval or rejection.
3. **Unsafe repairs could repeat the invention.** The same deterministic checks run on the rebuilt corrected pack before any new review call. A known-invalid replacement stops without paying for another review. Remaining semantic questions still require fresh, exhaustive final review. One bounded correction pass and existing call/budget ceilings remain unchanged.
4. **Old certificates could survive a policy change.** Admission and checkpointing require review policy 2. An old in-flight attempt cannot deliver under the new rules or restart paid work automatically. Its draft is preserved with an explicit recovery message. Previously saved history is not retroactively rewritten.
5. **Hashtag syntax hid assertions and CTAs.** Hashtags are no longer structural merely because they start with `#`. Known compound claims/imperatives are normalized consistently across case and separators; all hashtags need an explicit semantic decision. YouTube keyword items additionally require source context references. Neither references nor labels are represented as semantic proof.
6. **Editorial handling.** Aesthetic noun uses of "cover" are not curriculum verbs. Generation/review instructions retain optional examples, subjective copy and varied source-based repairs. The offline Fieldline correction supplies the nested video's missing example and removes layout-dependent scaffolding. Those manually scripted editorial improvements are not claimed as achieved by a live model.

No new service, dependency, model call or architecture layer was introduced. Full source statements and cross-format context accompany every batch. All 14 formats, image generation, editing/undo/export, account-scoped saves, quota/billing/Founder protections and the inactive post-launch retirement mechanism are retained.
