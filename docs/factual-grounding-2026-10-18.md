## Delivery boundary

The generation route uses `beginTextGenerationStage` and `checkpointTextGenerationStage` to persist a bounded draft, exhaustive review batches, at most one correction and fresh final review before atomic completion. `groundGeneration` remains the historical monolithic helper for frozen benchmark compatibility/tests, not the active route. This is not just a writing-prompt instruction. It also applies to targeted AI refinements. Durable admission, account binding, quota, replay and atomic history/usage protections remain. Unchecked or unresolved packs are not delivered, persisted in history or counted as successful generations.

1. Extract exact sentence/clause source statements from the original brief and the selected brand name, audience and offer. Retain source IDs, origin, field and domain tags. Saved-brand selection already copies those editable fields into the request; the reservation fingerprints this stable snapshot. Do not load unrelated account brands or silently substitute later brand edits.
2. Keep writing style and content goal as constraints, not factual evidence. Existing generated drafts are editing context only. Current brief takes precedence over conflicting brand context; unresolved ambiguity is not permission to invent a fact.
3. Enumerate original-offset text units across every requested format: pack title/summary, section title/platform/body/CTA, every item, email subject lines and nested repurposed assets. No text-bearing generated field is intentionally omitted.
4. Run separate structured semantic review batches of at most 48 units, each with the entire pack and complete source ledger. Every unit needs exactly one decision: source-supported, creative, subjective, structural or unsupported. Source-supported decisions require exact source references; compact IDs are expanded server-side to full original statements. Unsupported decisions carry a typed reason and a neutral/source-backed replacement. The reviewer considers contradictions across assets, not just individual strings.
5. Deterministic checks reject missing/duplicate/unknown decisions, nonexistent or fabricated quotations, certain numeric/currency mismatches, event-duration-to-outcome conversions, recognized engagement CTAs, certain stripped negations and counterfeit structural/subjective labels for concrete claims. Domain tags are not logical proof. Curriculum/capability paraphrases are not rejected merely for using different verbs.
6. Apply at most one correction pass to server-selected original offsets. Model-supplied paths cannot redirect edits. Reparse the whole pack against its format contract, then review the corrected pack afresh. Any remaining unsupported decision or invalid review fails closed; there is no third review or repeated regeneration.

Customer statements are **source-supported**, not independently verified facts. The implementation does not browse, invent evidence, or label customer statements externally verified. Exact quotation proves provenance, not truth or semantic entailment.

## Creativity and instructions

Optional illustrations, hypothetical experiments and subjective marketing language remain available. They cannot become actual curriculum, product capabilities, testimonials or measured outcomes. No CTA blocks engagement/contact/signup/purchase requests across assets, not merely the CTA property. Practical instructional steps and clearly framed self-evaluation are not automatically treated as engagement requests. A quoted product named "No CTA" does not change the selected mode; an explicit directive does.

Regression fixes include written-number versus numeral equivalence, unrelated duration versus price, currency/amount binding, thousands separators, clause-scoped negation, distinct product prices, and source-backed curriculum paraphrases. There is no hard-coded replacement pack or generic boilerplate fallback. A labelled placeholder is permitted by the reviewer only when neutral copy cannot work; it is subject to the same final review and format checks.

## Limits, failure and accounting

- One draft call, maximum 24,000 output tokens; at most two rounds of exhaustive review batches, 4,800 output tokens each. Maximum 23 provider calls per logical attempt, further constrained by exact-payload USD4 conservative admission accounting. No SDK retry, alternate provider/model or unbounded repair loop.
- Each provider stage has its own at-most-90-second budget inside the unchanged 120-second route. There is at most one provider call per request; successful stages checkpoint before any next request. No transaction stays open across provider networking.
- At most 500 review units, 160,000 serialized provider-request bytes and 180,000 response bytes per call. Existing source, format and result size limits remain. Oversized/truncated/refused/malformed reviews fail closed rather than skip validation.
- Durable per-stage metrics retain numeric token usage, bounds, timings and payload hashes. No source, review, output, credentials or account identifiers are added to logs. Missing or over-bound usage stops continuation.
- HTTP 202 means durable progress, not delivered content. HTTP 200 is only a completed/replayed validated result. Failed/unknown checkpoints return 502 (409 on a terminal lookup); uncertain persistence returns 503 and the same-ID recovery instruction. Operational failures do not blame missing customer facts.
- Terminal validation/provider failure releases application credit holds, not provider reservations or attempt throttles. Completed stages are never dispatched again. An unconfirmed post-dispatch crash becomes terminal after 120 seconds, without takeover or automatic retry. Committed checkpoints/final saves recover after lost acknowledgements.
- Studio preserves current edits and an account-scoped request snapshot, shows actual progress, and offers explicit same-attempt recovery for interruptions. Terminal attempts require an explicit new attempt; no automatic paid retry.
- Existing completed replays and historical/manual content are preserved, not retroactively certified or rewritten. Images, subscriptions and Founder entitlements are untouched.
